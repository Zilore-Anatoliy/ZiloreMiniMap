# Release notes for the new ZiloreMiniMap version (English)
# Lines starting with # are ignored. Sections: [New], [Improvements], [Fixes]

[New]
• Cumulative release.
• Silky-smooth full tactical map pan & zoom engine (60+ FPS even with all 8,000+ POIs enabled): single-blit RenderTargetBitmap interactive preview, O(1) matrix-transformed route/marker layer during gestures, and continuous cursor-anchored zoom across every animation frame.
• Unified modular update system (.zip Portable / Setup .exe / .exe) with "Update from File…", setup-sha256 verification, and ~950 MB less disk I/O per cycle.

[Improvements]
• Faster, smarter routing: lower-bound candidate pair sorting prunes ~75% of redundant A* searches, added a 16-entry route LRU cache (<0.1 ms repeat/mode-toggle lookups), and automatic sharp-bend (>=45 deg) turn extraction for rural hairpins.
• Quantized full-map POI clustering (1/6-octave steps) and dynamic BitmapCache toggling (zero double-buffering during pan/zoom, instant <0.2 ms sidebar scrolling while idle).
• Expanded direct/water/flight distance announcements (50 / 100 / 250 / 500 / 1000 / 2000 m).

[Fixes]
• Permanently resolved 1.5x sped-up speech and subsequent voice silence: speed detection now measures active vocal duration (excluding SAPI5 silence), heals both WAV and MP3 neural clips (including turns, sectors, and landmarks), locks native 16 kHz OneCore format, auto-recovers wedged TTS STA workers, and expires transient voice errors after 60 s.
• Fixed the initial 45 ms zoom hitch, sideways cursor drift during mouse-wheel bursts, and turn-check throttle reset on fresh GPS coordinates.
• Fixed Portable folder updates, Defender file-lock races, and orphaned %TEMP% helper folders.
